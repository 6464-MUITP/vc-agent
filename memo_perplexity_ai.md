### 3. Market Gaps / White‑Space

1. **Regulated‑industry search** – No player explicitly markets a HIPAA‑/GDPR‑ready, citation‑backed web search for healthcare or finance.  
2. **Hybrid citation + domain‑specific AI** – Combining real‑time web grounding with deep scholarly filters (Q‑ranking) is absent.  
3. **Transparent usage‑based pricing for developers** – Perplexity’s tiered flat fees vs. You.com’s per‑1k‑request model leaves a pricing clarity gap for high‑volume API consumers.  
4. **Open‑source / self‑hosted citation engine** – All offerings appear SaaS‑only; a self‑hostable, citation‑enabled engine could attract data‑sensitive enterprises.

---

### 4. What to Verify Before Relying on This Assessment

- **API availability & performance** – Existence, latency, and cost structure of Perplexity’s search API (not disclosed).  
- **Citation accuracy & freshness** – Independent benchmark of real‑time grounding vs. You.com’s “most up‑to‑date” claim.  
- **Compliance posture** – Data‑retention, encryption, HIPAA/GDPR certifications for Perplexity.  
- **Ad‑spend automation details** – Scope, limits, and replicability of the “Perplexity Computer” ad‑spend use case.  
- **Enterprise contract terms** – Custom pricing model and SLA commitments compared with competitors.
